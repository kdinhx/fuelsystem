PA-28-161 WARRIOR III • FUEL SYSTEM POH SPECS

Engine: Lycoming O-320-D3G, 160 BHP at 2700 RPM. 4-cylinder, direct-drive, horizontally-opposed, air-cooled.

Fuel Capacity: Total 50 U.S. Gal (25 Gal per wing). Usable: 48 U.S. Gal (24 Gal per wing). 17-Gallon visual indicator tab per tank (34 Gal total cruise load).

Carburetor: Marvel-Schebler MA-4SPA float carburetor. Operates at low pressure (0.5 to 8.0 PSI green arc).

Fuel Pumps: One engine-driven mechanical diaphragm fuel pump, supplemented by an auxiliary electric boost pump controlled by a rocker switch.

Electric Primer: A momentary push-button rocker switch energizes an electric solenoid valve on the firewall. The auxiliary electric boost pump must be running to pressurize fuel through the solenoid into intake ports on cylinders 1, 2, and 4.

Starting Procedure:
1. Fuel Selector → Desired Tank
2. Electric Aux Pump → ON (Check 5.8 PSI)
3. Electric Primer → PUSH & HOLD for 2 to 3 seconds
4. Throttle → Cracked 1/4 inch, Mixture → Full RICH
5. Ignition Switch → BOTH, engage STARTER
6. Aux Pump → OFF after starting to verify mechanical pump health
