Piper PA-28-161 Warrior III (160HP O-320-D3G)

Aeronautical POH Data & Factory Electric Primer System

Electric Primer Operation (POH §7.9)

The Warrior III replaces earlier Cherokee manual pump plungers with an electrical primer switch on the instrument panel. Turning the electric fuel pump ON builds system pressure; depressing the spring-loaded primer switch energizes a firewall-mounted electric solenoid valve that injects fuel directly into cylinders 1, 2, and 4.

Semi-Tapered Wings & 17-Gal Tab Fuel

50 US Gal total capacity (48 usable). Each wing contains a 25-gal tank equipped with an internal visual indicator collar tab at 17 gal (34 gal total usable for maximum payload balance).

Low-Pressure Marvel-Schebler MA-4SPA

Operates under low fuel pressure (0.5 to 8.0 PSI, nominal ~4.8 PSI). Powered by an engine-driven diaphragm mechanical pump supplemented by the Facet electric auxiliary boost pump.

Safety Detent Fuel Selector

3-position valve (LEFT, RIGHT, OFF). A mechanical spring latch locks out the OFF setting to prevent accidental in-flight engine starvation. Piper low-wings have no BOTH setting.
